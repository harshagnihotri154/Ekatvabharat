❖ Ekatvabharat Foundation 
 
 
 
About Us:- 
❖ Ekatvabharat Foundation incorporated as a Section 8 company in the year 2021 is striving hard to promote the well-being 
of humanity, eliminating extreme poverty and filling the opportunity gaps between rural and urban areas of India. 
❖ The mission of our organisation to empower Children, Youth and Women through programming and mobilization in the areas 
of health, environment, sports development, education, and livelihood with strategic partners. 
 
❖ Ekatvabharat Foundation works on various social aspects like women and child empowerment, education, healtcare, social jusustice 
and human rights.
