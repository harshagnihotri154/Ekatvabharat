Program Overview 
Online Programs 
Anyone who does not want to earn their Degrees sitting in classrooms – some want to work, 
others may not want to or be able to travel. Or Practicing professionals looking for career 
enrichment, progression and replacement. 
We offer a unique Bachelors Degree in Business Administration and Masters in Business 
Administration that can be earned through online mode of studies.

As against the traditional model of distance education, where self study is the main mode of 
learning, the program ensures student’s participation in virtual class, assist them with their 
queries and is conducted in a manner that keeps the interest level high. 
Online students connect with full campus learning experience by interacting with faculty 
members who are not only involved in full time delivery but industry professionals. Students 
will also have access to the latest learning technologies 
Why Online learning from Bharat Institute? 
 Flexibility – Study Any Place, Any Time 
 Employment – Degree / Graduation - Makes you more employable 
 Growth prospect – opens door to Promotions and better Increments 
 Support - Our faculty and staff will help you work towards achieving educational and 
professional success 
 Personalized Attention – Each year of this program includes 06 subjects, taught over a 
highly interactive 04 week span each, as against a fully 'self-study” based mode in other 
programs 
 Batch Allocation – Students are allotted batches with a maximum batch strength of 30 
with a dedicated faculty, maintaining a ratio of 1:30 
 Course Structure – Well developed and balanced structure ensures better learning and 
retention 
 Degree Recognition – Degree is from a UGC approved, NAAC Accredited University 
 
Career Opportunity in Hotel Management: 
 India Shining and so is the Hospitality sector (Hotels, Tourism, Retail & Events) 
 More than 1,000 new Hotels, Lodges, Guest houses coming up in the next 3 years 
 More than 4 lakh professionals required to manage these upcoming hotels, etc. 
 Government developing & promoting Tourism both for domestic & inbound travellers 
 India gearing up for hosting mega events 
 The diversity of experience in Hotel Management is greater than any other profession. 
Hotel industry involves combination of various skills like: 
o Hotel Management 
o Food & Beverage Service 
o Housekeeping & Facilities 
o Front Office Operation & Customer Relationship 
o Sales and Marketing 
o Human Resource & Training 
o Accounting. 
 Huge potential for young entrepreneurs to set up their own venture – budget hotels, 
restaurants, travel agencies, retail stores etc
